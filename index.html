About

About the company

Adams Nexus LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Wyoming, United States. It acts as the ownership and funding entity for our property activities: capital is held and allocated through Adams Nexus and invested into property operated under the Keyspace Property name.

Adams Nexus LLC does not sell products or services to the public. Its role is ownership, funding, and administrative oversight. Property sourcing, management, and day-to-day operations are carried out as Keyspace Property.
